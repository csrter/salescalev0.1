Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Salescale ("Salescale," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our advertising-management and customer-relationship platform, including our website, web application, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Who we are. Salescale is a business-to-business software platform that helps marketing agencies and their clients manage advertising campaigns, track conversions, and organize customer and lead data across connected advertising and business platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), organization details, and role, provided when you create or are invited to an account.
  • Connected-platform data: when you authorize a connection to a third-party advertising or business platform (such as Meta, Google, Microsoft Advertising, and similar providers), we access information from those platforms through their official APIs on your behalf. This may include ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ads, pages, business assets, performance metrics, conversion data, and lead-form submissions associated with the accounts you connect.
  • CRM and lead data: contact and company information you import, capture through connected lead forms and landing pages, or enter directly, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, and related attributes.
  • Usage and device data: log data, IP address, browser type, and interactions with the Services, collected automatically to operate and secure the platform.
  • Cookies: we use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to maintain session security. See Section 8.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Services.
  • To display advertising performance, metrics, and insights for the accounts you connect.
  • To create, edit, and manage advertising campaigns and conversions at your direction, subject to explicit confirmation for actions that spend money or change live campaigns.
  • To organize, enrich, and manage your CRM and lead data.
  • To authenticate users, secure accounts, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To communicate with you about your account, security, and service updates.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Platform Data From Meta and Other Providers

When you connect a Meta (Facebook/Instagram) account or another advertising or business platform, you grant Salescale permission to access data from that platform through its official API using access tokens you authorize. We use this data solely to provide the features you request within the Services for the connected account.

  • We store platform access and refresh tokens encrypted at rest and use them only to make API calls on your behalf.
  • We do not use platform data to build advertising profiles unrelated to the connected account, and we do not sell platform data.
  • We do not scrape any platform. Data is obtained only through officially supported APIs and the account holder's own authorization.
  • You may revoke a connection at any time from within the Services or from the provider's own settings, after which we stop accessing new data for that connection.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Our use of Meta Platform data adheres to the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers that host, store, and process data on our behalf (for example, cloud infrastructure and database providers), under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Within your organization, according to the roles and permissions you configure, and with client-facing recipients you explicitly grant access.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Salescale, our users, or the public.
  • In a business transfer, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing (including under the GDPR and CCPA).

To request access to or deletion of your data, or to have data associated with a connected platform removed, contact us at privacy@salescale.lol. We will verify your request and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. Deleting your account removes your personal data and revokes connected-platform access tokens; data deletion cascades to the records associated with your account.

7. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information, including encryption of sensitive credentials at rest, encrypted transport (TLS), access controls, and tenant isolation between organizations. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies and similar technologies to authenticate users and maintain session security. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the application. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling necessary cookies may prevent you from signing in.

9. International Transfers

We may process and store information in countries other than where you reside. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers of personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

The Services are intended for businesses and are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated through the Services or by email where appropriate.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at privacy@salescale.lol.
